Frequently Asked Questions

Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, a 3kW residential system is eligible for ₹78,000 direct bank transfer (DBT). Once our team completes installation, inspects with UPNEDA, and KESCo installs the net-meter, the subsidy is credited directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account within 30 to 45 days.
A bi-directional meter measures both the power you import from the KESCo grid and the surplus solar electricity you export during sunny daytime hours. RS Enterprises submits the technical feasibility application, arranges testing at KESCo lab, and coordinates the physical installation on your meter board.
A standard 3kW system requires approximately 250 to 300 square feet of shadow-free terrace area. A 5kW system requires about 450 to 500 square feet. We also build elevated galvanized structures so you can freely use your roof space beneath the panels.
Rooftop solar has zero moving parts. Routine maintenance simply requires wiping dust off the glass panels with clean water once every 10–14 days. RS Enterprises provides complimentary check-ups and structural inspections during the first year of operation.
